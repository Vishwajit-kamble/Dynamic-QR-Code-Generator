# PROJECT: TAPREVIEW
## Complete Full-Stack NFC & Dynamic QR Management Platform

Act as a senior full-stack software engineer, UI/UX designer, backend architect, database engineer and security specialist.

Build a complete, fully functional, production-ready web application called **TapReview**.

This is a private admin-only web application used by one business owner to manage pre-manufactured NFC Google Review cards.

Do not build a customer-facing SaaS platform. There are no customer accounts, subscriptions or business-owner dashboards.

The application must have a polished frontend, working backend, persistent database, dynamic QR redirection, NFC URL support, card inventory management and scan analytics.

Do not create just a frontend prototype. All buttons, forms, filters, navigation, database operations and business workflows must actually work.

---

# 1. BUSINESS MODEL AND CORE REQUIREMENTS

The business owner manufactures NFC review cards in advance.

Initial inventory: 50 physical NFC cards.

Every physical card has:
- A unique permanent card ID.
- A unique preprinted QR code.
- An NFC chip already installed.
- A unique TapReview redirect URL.

Example:

Card TR001:
https://yourdomain.com/r/TR001

Card TR002:
https://yourdomain.com/r/TR002

Card TR003:
https://yourdomain.com/r/TR003

The QR code and NFC chip on each card must contain exactly the same URL.

The cards are manufactured before being sold.

The admin visits local businesses personally and carries ready-made cards.

When a business purchases a card, the admin:
1. Opens the private TapReview dashboard.
2. Selects an available physical card.
3. Enters the business name.
4. Enters its Google Review URL.
5. Optionally enters phone number, address and notes.
6. Activates the card.
7. Delivers the card to the business.

The card immediately redirects customers to that business's Google Review destination.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES:

- One card can belong to only one business.
- Once activated, a card is permanently assigned.
- No reassignment of activated cards.
- No deleting or changing the permanent card ID.
- No changing the preprinted QR URL.
- Google Review destination can be updated if the same business changes its review link.
- Deactivation must be supported.
- Reactivation is permitted only for the original business.
- Every business must have a separate card.
- Never send customers to a business-selection page.
- No customer login or registration.
- No artificial or fabricated reviews.
- Never automatically submit reviews on behalf of customers.

# 8. DYNAMIC QR REDIRECT SYSTEM

This is the core functionality.

Every card has a unique permanent redirect URL.

Example:

https://yourdomain.com/r/TR001

The QR code contains this URL.

The NFC chip contains this same URL.

When a customer scans or taps:

1. Flask receives the request.
2. Extract the card ID from the URL.
3. Look up the card in Supabase.
4. Check whether the card is ACTIVATED.
5. Check whether the assigned business exists.
6. Retrieve that business's Google Review URL.
7. Record a scan event.
8. Redirect the customer to the destination.

Use an HTTP 302 redirect so the destination can change without changing the printed QR code.

If the card is AVAILABLE:
Display a simple "This card is not activated yet" page.

If the card is DEACTIVATED:
Display a friendly "This review card is currently inactive" page.

If the card ID does not exist:
Return a proper 404 page.

IMPORTANT:
Never redirect to an arbitrary URL supplied through a query parameter. Always retrieve the trusted destination from the database.
